## Intended use and limits
- Research prototype: indicative freshness level of raw chicken photographed in THE SAME dark box, LEDs, camera and settings as the training data. Not a food-safety device.
- Labels are temperature-equivalent hours since filming started, not microbial counts; glints are specular reflections (moisture), not bacteria.
- Only 9 pieces; the `spoiled_risk` level comes from 2 overnight pieces only. CIs are wide; new pieces/setups may fall outside the training range (the OOD check abstains then).
- Unknown storage history before filming is not modelled (open questions 2, 10).
