## Problems hit and fixes
- A session-median robust z with raw MADs (≈ 0.5 px, 0.05 % area) flagged sub-percent jitter, the whole post-step block of D2A BLUE, and D2B settling. Fix: centred rolling median plus per-feature MAD floors (config, with justification).
- The BLUE mask leak in D1A gave z = 32 on the centroid. Fix: geometry features for `roi.ref_lights` only, plus ROI intensity for all lights.
- `sroi_median` of uint8 is quantised (one grey level ≈ 8 % on dim BLUE) and produced the highest z values. Switched to `sroi_mean`.
- Step tie-break: a clean step ties at i−1, i, i+1, and argmax picked i−1. Now the middle of the tied maxima is used.
- The roi stage selected frames on the final `qc_status`, which would drop scene-change rejects on a re-run (not idempotent). Added `qc_pre_roi`.
- At 300 dpi the sheets were 3.5 MB each (git-tracked). Added `qa.dpi` = 150 (2 MB each) and no SVG for the sheets.
- ruff E501 and a format issue: fixed.
