## Design decisions / deviations (with evidence)
1. **Multi-Otsu instead of plain Otsu.** Plain Otsu on the real frames kept only the brightly lit half of the meat (LED gradient). The lowest of 3 multi-Otsu thresholds, floored at `roi.min_level` = 8 (= qc.min_signal), covers the lit surface. The floor stops Otsu splitting background noise.
2. **One session ROI from RED frames, used for all lights.** Per-frame mask IoU vs a RED+GREEN reference (prototype on all 919 frames): RED 0.90–0.98, GREEN 0.66–0.95, BLUE 0.34–0.87. BLUE and GREEN leak into the lit tray and holder. RED IoU is flat over each session (e.g. D3A quartile means 0.926/0.932/0.931/0.920 over 5.7 h), so the meat does not move and a fixed ROI is valid. It also makes the R, G and B values of a triplet come from the same pixels. Config `roi.ref_lights: [RED]`.
3. **Holder/tray exclusion (`exclude_foreign`).** The first full run showed the D3A session ROI including the bright blue holder at the bottom. The holder's RED (~18–20) is above the threshold, but its session-median BLUE/RED is 1.5–3 vs 0.3–0.45 for meat. Regions with BLUE/RED > 1.0 that touch the ROI edge and are ≥ 50 px are removed and grown by erode_px. Interior bluish specks are kept (glint streaks, the shadowed lower side of D1B). Removed: D1A 0, D1B 5,231, D2A 10,586, D2B 4,692, D3A 11,094 px.
4. **Per-frame masks are QC only.** `roi_ok=False` (E_NO_MEAT_FOUND) if the frame's own segmentation fails or its session has no ROI. `W_ROI_LOW_IOU` warning only for ref-light frames with IoU < roi.min_iou (0.6). Non-ref lights are not judged on IoU.
5. **Glint top-hat with outside-ROI fill.** The first test run found 4 false glints at the ROI rectangle's corners (the top-hat sees the meat/background border as a peak). Pixels outside the ROI are now set to the ROI median before the top-hat.
6. **Merge/split rule** (P2.T2 text made exact): 2+ prev glints whose nearest current glint is the same one, each within match_px + r_eq(current), and area(current) ≥ merge_area_ratio (1.5) × the largest part. Split is symmetric. The rest are Hungarian-matched (distance ≤ match_px → persist); leftovers are births/deaths.
7. ROI stage does not compute glints; glint features come in P4 and glint overlays in P2.T5.
